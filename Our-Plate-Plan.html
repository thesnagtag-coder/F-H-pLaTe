Our Plate Plan

High protein, high fibre, low carb - beans every day. Portions for F and H.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Plate: based on the American Diabetes Association (ADA) Diabetes Plate Method for a 23 cm plate - half non-starchy vegetables, a quarter protein, a quarter carbohydrate foods (here, mostly beans).
  • Energy estimates use the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. F: 55, 1.70 m, 95 kg, sedentary (x1.2) = about 1,890 kcal to maintain, minus about 500 kcal = about 1,400 kcal. H: 56, 1.73 m, 78 kg, walks a lot (x1.375 to x1.55) = about 2,180-2,460 kcal to maintain, minus about 300-500 kcal = about 1,900-2,000 kcal. These are estimates - adjust by results.
  • Protein: 1.2-1.6 g per kg of body weight, a range widely used in weight-loss research to help protect muscle. F worked on goal weight (75 kg = 90-120 g), H on current weight (78 kg = 94-125 g).
  • Fibre: 30 g a day for adults (UK Scientific Advisory Committee on Nutrition, 2015).
  • Waist: keep waist to less than half your height (NICE guidance, 2022).
  • H's glucose: ADA target is under 10.0 mmol/L (180 mg/dL) 1-2 hours after starting a meal. His doctor may set a different target - follow theirs.
  • Eating order: food-order studies (e.g. Shukla et al., Diabetes Care, 2015) found that eating vegetables and protein before carbohydrates lowered the rise in glucose after meals.
  • Whey protein powder: values from the Optimum Nutrition Gold Standard 100% Whey (vanilla) tub label - 31 g scoop = 126 kcal, 24 g protein, 4 g carbs, 1.5 g fat.
  • Nutrition values: USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy) per 100 g, cooked or drained weight unless stated. Estimates only - brands vary, so check labels.
  • Carbs are total carbohydrate including fibre. Net carbs = total carbs minus fibre. UK and EU labels usually list carbs without fibre already, so compare like with like.
  • Kitchen measures: 1 tsp = 5 ml, 1 tbsp = 15 ml.
  • Beans safety: dried red kidney beans must be soaked, then boiled hard for at least 10 minutes before simmering (NHS) - never cook them from dry in a slow cooker. Canned beans are already cooked.
  • Batch-cooked beans and leftovers: cool and refrigerate within 2 hours, keep at 5°C or below and eat within 3-4 days, or freeze (USDA FSIS).
  • Share these portions with your doctor or dietitian, especially as H takes diabetes medication.
